# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

**Contract No.: LSE-2023-2210**

**Effective Date: October 1, 2023**

---

## 1. PARTIES

This Commercial Lease Agreement (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of October 1, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**GreenStone Logistics Properties LLC**, a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Georgia, with its principal office at 3300 Peachtree Industrial Boulevard, Duluth, Georgia 30096 ("**Landlord**"); and

**Apex Legal Solutions LLP**, a limited liability partnership registered under the laws of the State of Georgia, with its principal office at 1200 Abernathy Road NE, Suite 500, Atlanta, Georgia 30328 ("**Tenant**").

Landlord and Tenant are each individually referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**."

---

## 2. RECITALS

WHEREAS, Landlord is the owner of certain commercial office premises located at 3300 Peachtree Industrial Boulevard, Building 4, Suite 210, Duluth, Georgia 30096, comprising approximately 8,750 rentable square feet (the "**Premises**"), as more particularly described in **Exhibit A**;

WHEREAS, Tenant desires to lease the Premises from Landlord for use as general professional offices;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 3. DEFINITIONS

3.1 "**Additional Rent**" means all sums, other than Base Rent, payable by Tenant under this Lease, including Operating Expenses and Taxes.

3.2 "**Base Rent**" means the amounts set forth in Section 6.1.

3.3 "**Building**" means the office building located at 3300 Peachtree Industrial Boulevard, Duluth, Georgia 30096, of which the Premises form a part.

3.4 "**Commencement Date**" means November 1, 2023.

3.5 "**Common Areas**" means all areas of the Building and the underlying real property designated by Landlord for common use, including lobbies, corridors, parking areas, and landscaped areas.

3.6 "**Lease Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

3.7 "**Operating Expenses**" means all reasonable costs of operating, maintaining, and repairing the Building and Common Areas, as further described in **Exhibit B**.

3.8 "**Permitted Use**" means general professional and administrative office use, and no other use, without Landlord's prior written consent.

3.9 "**Security Deposit**" means the amount set forth in Section 6.5.

---

## 4. PREMISES

4.1 Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the Premises, together with the non-exclusive right to use the Common Areas, subject to the terms and conditions of this Lease.

4.2 The Premises are leased in "as-is" condition, except for the Landlord's Work described in **Exhibit C**, which Landlord shall substantially complete prior to the Commencement Date.

4.3 Tenant shall have the non-exclusive right to use twenty-six (26) parking spaces in the Building's surface parking lot, at no additional charge during the initial Lease Term.

---

## 5. TERM

5.1 **Lease Term**. The term of this Lease shall commence on the Commencement Date and continue for a period of sixty (60) months, expiring on October 31, 2028, unless earlier terminated or extended in accordance with this Lease (the "**Lease Term**").

5.2 **Renewal Option**. Provided Tenant is not in **material breach** of this Lease, Tenant shall have one (1) option to renew the Lease for an additional term of five (5) years, at ninety-five percent (95%) of the then-prevailing fair market rental rate, exercisable by written notice to Landlord not less than one hundred eighty (180) days prior to expiration of the initial Lease Term.

5.3 **Holdover**. If Tenant remains in possession of the Premises after expiration of the Lease Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall be deemed a tenant at will at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Rent in effect immediately prior to expiration, and shall otherwise remain subject to all terms of this Lease.

---

## 6. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS

6.1 **Base Rent**. Tenant shall pay Base Rent according to the following schedule:

| Lease Year | Annual Base Rent | Monthly Installment |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 (Nov 2023 – Oct 2024) | $236,250 | $19,687.50 |
| Year 2 (Nov 2024 – Oct 2025) | $243,338 | $20,278.13 |
| Year 3 (Nov 2025 – Oct 2026) | $250,637 | $20,886.46 |
| Year 4 (Nov 2026 – Oct 2027) | $258,157 | $21,513.04 |
| Year 5 (Nov 2027 – Oct 2028) | $265,901 | $22,158.44 |

6.2 **Payment**. Base Rent shall be payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month, without notice, demand, deduction, or offset, to the account designated by Landlord in **Exhibit D**.

6.3 **Late Charges**. Any Base Rent or Additional Rent not received within five (5) days of the due date shall incur a late charge of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount, plus interest at the rate of twelve percent (12%) per annum until paid.

6.4 **Additional Rent**. Tenant shall pay its proportionate share (estimated at 22.4%, based on the Premises' rentable square footage relative to the Building) of Operating Expenses and Taxes, billed monthly on an estimated basis with an annual reconciliation, as further described in Exhibit B.

6.5 **Security Deposit**. Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of **USD $39,375** as a Security Deposit, to be held by Landlord as security for Tenant's performance of its obligations hereunder, and returned (less any lawful deductions) within thirty (30) days after the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease and surrender of the Premises.

---

## 7. USE OF PREMISES

7.1 Tenant shall use the Premises solely for the Permitted Use and shall not use or permit the Premises to be used for any other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

7.2 Tenant shall comply with all applicable laws, ordinances, and regulations pertaining to its use and occupancy of the Premises, including the Americans with Disabilities Act and all applicable fire and safety codes.

7.3 Tenant shall not commit or permit any waste or nuisance on the Premises, and shall not use the Premises in any manner that would invalidate or increase the premiums for any insurance carried by Landlord.

---

## 8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

8.1 **Landlord's Obligations**. Landlord shall maintain and repair, in good working order, the structural elements of the Building, the roof, exterior walls, foundation, and Building systems serving the Premises (including HVAC, plumbing, and electrical systems), except for damage caused by Tenant's negligence or misuse.

8.2 **Tenant's Obligations**. Tenant shall, at its own expense, maintain the interior, non-structural portions of the Premises in good condition, including fixtures, flooring, and interior partitions installed by Tenant, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

8.3 **Alterations**. Tenant shall not make any structural alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Non-structural alterations costing less than $25,000 in the aggregate per Lease Year may be made without Landlord's consent, provided Tenant gives Landlord prior written notice.

---

## 9. INSURANCE

9.1 **Tenant's Insurance**. Tenant shall maintain, at its own expense, throughout the Lease Term: (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate; (b) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and improvements on a full replacement cost basis; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by applicable law.

9.2 **Landlord's Insurance**. Landlord shall maintain commercial property insurance covering the Building on a full replacement cost basis, and commercial general liability insurance covering the Common Areas.

9.3 **Waiver of Subrogation**. Each Party waives, and shall cause its insurers to waive, any right of subrogation against the other Party for losses covered by the insurance required under this Section 9.

---

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **Tenant's Indemnification**. Tenant shall **indemnify**, defend, and hold harmless Landlord from and against any claims, damages, liabilities, and reasonable expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of: (a) Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises; (b) any negligent act or omission of Tenant or its employees, contractors, or invitees; or (c) any breach of this Lease by Tenant.

10.2 **Landlord's Indemnification**. Landlord shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Tenant from and against any claims, damages, liabilities, and reasonable expenses arising out of: (a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees or agents; or (b) Landlord's breach of this Lease.

10.3 **Survival**. The indemnification obligations under this Section 10 shall survive expiration or termination of this Lease.

---

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY ARISING FROM A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 10, GROSS NEGLIGENCE, OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, OR **CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES** ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS LEASE, INCLUDING LOSS OF BUSINESS PROFITS.

11.2 Landlord's aggregate liability to Tenant under this Lease shall not exceed the total Base Rent paid by Tenant during the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim (the "**Liability Cap**"), except in cases of Landlord's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or Landlord's indemnification obligations under Section 10.2.

---

## 12. DAMAGE AND DESTRUCTION

12.1 If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty such that they cannot be restored within one hundred eighty (180) days, either Party may terminate this Lease upon written notice within thirty (30) days of the casualty event.

12.2 If the Premises can be restored within one hundred eighty (180) days, Landlord shall proceed with restoration with reasonable diligence, and Base Rent shall abate proportionately during the period of restoration to the extent the Premises are rendered untenantable.

---

## 13. CONDEMNATION

13.1 If the whole or a substantial part of the Premises is taken by eminent domain such that the remainder is not reasonably suitable for Tenant's use, either Party may terminate this Lease effective as of the date of taking.

---

## 14. FORCE MAJEURE

14.1 Except for Tenant's obligation to pay Base Rent and Additional Rent, neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Lease due to a **Force Majeure Event**, including acts of God, fire, earthquake, war, governmental restriction, labor disputes, or shortages of materials, provided the affected Party gives prompt written notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

---

## 15. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

15.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any portion of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Tenant may assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to an Affiliate of Tenant without Landlord's consent, upon prior written notice to Landlord.

15.2 No assignment or subletting shall relieve Tenant of its obligations under this Lease unless Landlord expressly releases Tenant in writing.

---

## 16. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

16.1 **Events of Default**. Each of the following shall constitute an event of default: (a) Tenant's failure to pay Base Rent or Additional Rent within five (5) days after written notice of non-payment; (b) Tenant's failure to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice, or such longer period as is reasonably necessary provided Tenant commences cure within such period and diligently pursues completion; or (c) Tenant's insolvency or filing for bankruptcy protection.

16.2 **Landlord's Remedies**. Upon an uncured event of default, Landlord may, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity: (a) terminate this Lease and recover possession of the Premises; (b) recover all unpaid Base Rent and Additional Rent, plus interest and late charges; and (c) recover the present value of the remaining Base Rent for the balance of the Lease Term, less the fair market rental value of the Premises for such period, subject to Landlord's duty to mitigate damages under applicable Georgia law.

16.3 **Tenant's Remedies**. If Landlord fails to perform any material obligation under this Lease and such failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant (or such longer period as is reasonably necessary), Tenant may, in addition to any other remedy available at law, pursue self-help remedies with prior written notice and offset reasonable costs incurred against future Base Rent, subject to a cap of one (1) month's Base Rent per occurrence.

---

## 17. GOVERNING LAW

17.1 This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Georgia, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

17.2 The Parties agree that the state courts located in Gwinnett County, Georgia shall have exclusive **jurisdiction** over any dispute arising out of or relating to this Lease, except as provided in Section 18.

---

## 18. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

18.1 In the event of any dispute arising out of or relating to this Lease, the Parties shall first attempt to resolve the dispute through good-faith negotiation for a period of fifteen (15) days.

18.2 If unresolved, either Party may submit the dispute to binding **arbitration** administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, to be conducted in Atlanta, Georgia before a single arbitrator, except that either Party may seek **injunctive relief** or an unlawful detainer action in the courts identified in Section 17.2 to recover possession of the Premises.

## 20. GENERAL PROVISIONS

20.1 **Entire Agreement**. This Lease, together with all Exhibits attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the Premises and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral.

20.2 **Amendment**. This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

20.3 **Severability**. If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

20.4 **Quiet Enjoyment**. Landlord covenants that, so long as Tenant is not in default, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly hold and enjoy the Premises during the Lease Term.

20.5 **Estoppel Certificates**. Each Party shall, within fifteen (15) days of written request, execute an estoppel certificate confirming the status of this Lease.

20.6 **Subordination**. This Lease is subject and subordinate to any mortgage now or hereafter placed on the Building, provided Tenant receives a commercially reasonable non-disturbance agreement from any such mortgagee.

20.7 **Counterparts**. This Lease may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
