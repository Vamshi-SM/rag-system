II. CFO FINANCIAL UPDATE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Michael Anders presented the Q3 2024 financial results. Revenue for the
quarter was $61.4 million, representing 8% year-over-year growth.
Michael Anders noted that the pending litigation matter against GreenStone
Logistics Corp. (Case ID LC-2045) remains reserved on the balance sheet
at $2.1 million, consistent with outside counsel's risk assessment
provided by Apex Legal Solutions on September 11, 2024.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
III. PROJECT PHOENIX - PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF BLUEPEAK SOFTWARE, INC.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
James Thornton, Esq. presented a summary of the due diligence findings
and definitive agreement terms for the proposed acquisition of BluePeak
Software, Inc. (see Due Diligence Report, Matter Number APX-2024-0201,
dated September 20, 2024, and Merger & Acquisition Review Memorandum of
even date herewith).

Key points presented to the Board:
1. Purchase price of $128,000,000, subject to working capital adjustment.
2. Financing consisting of $60,000,000 cash on hand and a $70,000,000
   term loan facility with Orion Financial.
3. All six closing conditions identified in due diligence have been
   satisfied as of the date of this meeting, including resolution of the
   security incident remediation item (Finding DD-011) and the Texas
   Attorney General inquiry (Finding DD-008).
4. Sterling Consulting's fairness opinion, delivered October 1, 2024,
   concluding the purchase price is fair to NovaTech Systems from a
   financial point of view.

Nicole Farrow asked about the open-source licensing issue identified in
due diligence (Finding DD-006). James Thornton confirmed that a 90-day
post-closing remediation plan is in place and that the risk is covered
by the transaction's indemnification and escrow provisions.

Dr. Samuel Ibe inquired about integration timeline and retention of key
BluePeak Software personnel, including CEO Robert Nakamura. Lisa Manning
confirmed that a $2,400,000 retention bonus pool has been structured for
12 key employees, with agreements to be finalized before closing.

RESOLVED: Upon motion duly made by Patricia Alvarez and seconded by
Dr. Samuel Ibe, the Board unanimously approved the Stock Purchase
Agreement for the acquisition of BluePeak Software, Inc. on the terms
presented, and authorized Michael Anders and Wen Zhao to execute all
documents necessary to consummate the transaction, with closing targeted
for October 31, 2024.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IV. LITIGATION UPDATE - NOVATECH SYSTEMS V. GREENSTONE LOGISTICS CORP.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
James Thornton provided an update on Case ID LC-2045, pending before
Hon. Margaret Ellison in the U.S. District Court for the Northern
District of Illinois. He reported that cross-motions for summary
judgment were filed on January 15, 2025 (note: this update was previewed
to the Board based on anticipated filings, as the litigation team's
strategy memo had been circulated in advance of this meeting) and that
mediation in January 2025 did not result in settlement, with GreenStone
Logistics' final offer of $1.2 million rejected as inadequate relative to
the $4,010,000 damages figure calculated by the client's retained expert,
Dr. Helena Marsh.

Wen Zhao asked whether the litigation posed any risk to the BluePeak
Software transaction, given that GreenStone Logistics is also a customer
of BluePeak Software under Contract No. BP-GS-2021-0056. James Thornton
confirmed the two matters are unrelated and that GreenStone Logistics
had already consented to the change of control under that contract on
September 30, 2024.

The Board took no action on this item and directed management to continue
monitoring the litigation.
