APPLICATION SUMMARY
Atlas BioTech seeks patent protection for a novel microfluidic sensor
array capable of continuous, non-invasive glucose biomarker detection
through interstitial fluid sampling. The invention builds on prior
provisional application No. 63/098,442, filed June 4, 2025, and includes
three independent claims and eighteen dependent claims.

CLAIMS OVERVIEW
Claim 1 (independent): A microfluidic sensor array comprising a
biocompatible membrane layer, an electrochemical detection unit, and a
wireless transmission module configured to relay glucose concentration
data at intervals of no greater than five minutes.

Claim 8 (independent): A method for calibrating the sensor array using a
machine-learning correction model trained on interstitial fluid variance
data.

Claim 15 (independent): A system integrating the sensor array with a
companion mobile application for real-time alerting of hypoglycemic and
hyperglycemic events.
