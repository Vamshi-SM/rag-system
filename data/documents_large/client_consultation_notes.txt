ENTRY 3: REDWOOD HEALTHCARE - DATA PRIVACY CONSULTATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Date: 2024-04-11
Client: Redwood Healthcare
Client ID: CLT-5502
Matter Number: APX-2024-0188
Client Contact: Dr. Amanda Foster, Chief Compliance Officer
Client Address: 2200 Cascade Avenue, Portland, OR 97201
Client Email: a.foster@redwoodhealthcare.org
Client Phone: (503) 555-6674
Attorney Present: Rachel Kim, Esq.; Elena Rodriguez, Esq. (Data Privacy
Practice Group)

Summary: Dr. Foster requested guidance on Redwood Healthcare's obligations
under both HIPAA and, due to a newly launched telehealth service targeting
patients in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation
(GDPR). Client operates 14 clinical facilities across Oregon and
Washington and is preparing to launch "Project Meridian," a telehealth
platform expansion.

Discussion Points:
- Reviewed data flow diagrams for the telehealth platform; identified that
  patient data from EU-based users would be processed on servers located
  in the United States, triggering GDPR Article 44 (cross-border transfer)
  obligations.
- Client confirmed no existing Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) are in
  place with its cloud hosting vendor, BluePeak Software.
- Advised client that a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) should be
  completed prior to launch, targeted for June 1, 2024.
- Discussed potential HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) gaps with
  three subcontractors identified during the meeting: BluePeak Software,
  MedConnect Data Systems, and CarePath Analytics.
- See Legal Research Notes (Matter Number APX-2024-0188) for detailed GDPR
  and HIPAA statutory analysis prepared following this consultation.

Action Items:
1. [Elena Rodriguez] Draft DPIA template and coordinate with client's IT
   department - Due 2024-04-25.
2. [Rachel Kim] Request copies of existing BAAs with BluePeak Software,
   MedConnect Data Systems, and CarePath Analytics - Due 2024-04-18.
3. [Client - Dr. Foster] Provide data flow diagrams in editable format -
   Due 2024-04-15.

Risk Note: Client's telehealth launch date of July 1, 2024 is at risk if
BAA gaps are not resolved by June 15, 2024. Recommend client consider a
phased launch limited to non-EU patients if timeline slips. See
Regulatory Compliance Checklist, Matter Number APX-2024-0188, item
RCC-014, for detailed tracking.
