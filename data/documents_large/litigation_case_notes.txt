I. CASE BACKGROUND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NovaTech Systems, Inc. ("NovaTech" or "Client") entered into a Master Services
Agreement with GreenStone Logistics Corp. ("GreenStone") on June 14, 2022,
Contract No. MSA-NT-GS-2022-0087, under which GreenStone agreed to provide
warehousing and last-mile distribution services for NovaTech's hardware
division across the Midwest region.

Client alleges that beginning in Q3 2023, GreenStone repeatedly failed to
meet the service-level commitments set forth in Schedule C (Delivery
Performance Standards) of the MSA, specifically Section 14.2 (Late Delivery
Remedies) and Section 9.5 (Warehouse Damage Liability). Client further
alleges that GreenStone's failures caused approximately $4,250,000 in lost
sales, expedited freight costs, and customer chargebacks.

Client seeks damages under a breach of contract theory, and in the
alternative, under a theory of negligent performance. Client also seeks
attorneys' fees pursuant to Section 22.1 of the MSA (Prevailing Party Fee
Shifting Clause).

VI. RISK ASSESSMENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Overall Case Risk Rating: MODERATE

Strengths:
- Internal GreenStone emails documenting known staffing shortfalls provide
  strong evidence of breach and negate the force majeure defense for the
  bulk of the claim period (August-December 2023, prior to the December
  winter storms).
- Deposition testimony from Daniel Cross corroborates staffing shortfall
  allegations.

Weaknesses:
- Defendant's force majeure defense may reduce damages attributable to the
  December 2023 winter storm period (approximately $380,000 of the claimed
  damages).
- Damages experts remain significantly apart ($4,010,000 vs. $610,000);
  this gap increases trial unpredictability.

Recommendation: Continue toward summary judgment on the Section 9.5 claim
while remaining open to a second mediation session following the summary
judgment ruling. Realistic settlement range: $2.1 million to $2.8 million.
