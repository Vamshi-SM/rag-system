# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

**Contract No.: EMP-2024-0339**

**Effective Date: February 1, 2024**

## 1. PARTIES

This Employment Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Orion Financial Group, Inc.**, a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Illinois, with its principal place of business at 220 South Wacker Drive, Suite 1400, Chicago, Illinois 60606 ("**Company**"); and

**Ms. Natalie Ann Whitcombe**, an individual residing at 1147 Cedar Grove Lane, Evanston, Illinois 60201 ("**Employee**").

Company and Employee are each individually referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**."

---

## 2. RECITALS

WHEREAS, Company wishes to employ Employee as **Director of Risk Analytics**, and Employee wishes to accept such employment, on the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 3. DEFINITIONS

3.1 "**Base Salary**" means the annual salary set forth in Section 6.1.

3.2 "**Cause**" has the meaning set forth in Section 12.3.

3.3 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.1.

3.4 "**Good Reason**" has the meaning set forth in Section 12.4.

3.5 "**Intellectual Property**" means all inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, improvements, processes, and other work product conceived, developed, or reduced to practice by Employee during the course of employment.

3.6 "**Restricted Period**" means the period of employment plus twelve (12) months following termination of employment for any reason.

3.7 "**Territory**" means the United States of America.

---

## 4. POSITION AND DUTIES

4.1 **Position**. Company hereby employs Employee, and Employee hereby accepts employment, as Director of Risk Analytics, reporting to the Chief Risk Officer, Mr. Devon Marsh.

4.2 **Duties**. Employee shall perform such duties as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be reasonably assigned by Company from time to time, including oversight of the enterprise risk modeling team and liaison with Company's external auditors.

4.3 **Full-Time Commitment**. Employee shall devote substantially all of her business time, attention, and **reasonable efforts** to the performance of her duties and shall not engage in any other employment or business activity that conflicts with her duties hereunder without the prior written consent of Company.

4.4 **Location**. Employee's principal place of work shall be Company's Chicago office, subject to Company's hybrid work policy permitting up to two (2) remote working days per week.

---

## 5. TERM

5.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated by either Party in accordance with Section 12 (the "**Employment Term**"). Employee's employment is at-will, except as modified by the specific severance provisions of Section 13.

---

## 6. COMPENSATION

6.1 **Base Salary**. Company shall pay Employee an annual base salary of **USD $178,500**, payable in accordance with Company's standard payroll practices, less applicable withholdings (the "**Base Salary**").

6.2 **Annual Bonus**. Employee shall be eligible for an annual discretionary performance bonus targeted at thirty percent (30%) of Base Salary, subject to Company and individual performance criteria established by the Compensation Committee, payable no later than March 15 of the year following the applicable performance year.

6.3 **Sign-On Bonus**. Company shall pay Employee a one-time sign-on bonus of **USD $15,000**, payable within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date, subject to repayment on a pro-rata basis if Employee resigns without Good Reason within twelve (12) months of the Effective Date.

6.4 **Equity**. Employee shall be granted restricted stock units with a grant-date value of **USD $60,000**, vesting in equal annual installments over four (4) years, subject to the terms of Company's Equity Incentive Plan and a separate award agreement.

6.5 **Benefits**. Employee shall be eligible to participate in Company's health, dental, vision, retirement, and other benefit plans on the same terms as similarly situated employees, subject to the terms of such plans as may be amended from time to time.

6.6 **Paid Time Off**. Employee shall accrue twenty-two (22) days of paid time off per calendar year, in accordance with Company's PTO policy.

6.7 **Business Expenses**. Company shall reimburse Employee for reasonable and documented business expenses incurred in the performance of her duties, in accordance with Company's expense reimbursement policy, within thirty (30) days of submission.

---

## 7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Employee represents and warrants that: (a) she is not subject to any employment agreement, restrictive covenant, or other obligation that would prevent or restrict her from performing her duties under this Agreement; (b) she has disclosed to Company any non-competition or non-solicitation obligations owed to any prior employer; and (c) the information provided by her in connection with her application for employment is true, accurate, and complete in all material respects.

7.2 Company represents and warrants that it has the requisite corporate authority to enter into this Agreement.

---

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

8.1 **Assignment**. Employee hereby assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in and to any Intellectual Property conceived, created, or developed by Employee, solely or jointly with others, during the Employment Term and within the scope of her employment, or using Company's equipment, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "**Company IP**").

8.2 **Disclosure**. Employee shall promptly disclose to Company all Company IP and shall execute all documents reasonably necessary to perfect Company's ownership thereof, both during and after the Employment Term.

8.3 **Moral Rights**. To the extent permitted by applicable law, Employee waives all moral rights in the Company IP.

8.4 **Excluded Inventions**. This Section 8 does not apply to any invention that Employee developed entirely on her own time without using Company's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information, unless such invention relates to Company's business or actual or demonstrably anticipated research. Employee has disclosed all such excluded inventions, if any, in **Appendix C**.

---

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Definition**. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Company's business, including but not limited to risk models, financial data, client lists, pricing methodologies, personal data of clients and employees, trade secrets, and business strategies, whether disclosed before or during the Employment Term.

9.2 **Obligations**. Employee shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without Company's prior written consent; and (c) use Confidential Information solely in connection with the performance of her duties for Company.

9.3 **Return of Materials**. Upon termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall promptly return to Company all documents, records, and other materials containing Confidential Information, including all copies thereof, whether in physical or electronic form.

9.4 **Survival**. The obligations set forth in this Section 9 shall survive termination of this Agreement indefinitely with respect to trade secrets, and for a period of five (5) years with respect to other Confidential Information.

---

## 10. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

10.1 **Non-Competition**. During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, within the Territory, engage in or provide services substantially similar to those she provided to Company to any business that directly competes with Company's risk analytics or financial advisory services, without the prior written consent of Company.

10.2 **Non-Solicitation of Clients**. During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not directly or indirectly solicit or induce any client of Company with whom Employee had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding termination to reduce or terminate its business with Company.

10.3 **Non-Solicitation of Employees**. During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not directly or indirectly solicit or induce any employee of Company to terminate their employment with Company.

10.4 **Reasonableness**. Employee acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 10 are reasonable in scope, geography, and duration, and are necessary to protect Company's legitimate business interests, including its Confidential Information and client relationships.

10.5 **Blue Pencil**. If any court of competent jurisdiction determines that any restriction in this Section 10 is unenforceable as written, the Parties intend that such restriction be modified to the minimum extent necessary to render it enforceable.

---

## 11. DATA PROTECTION

11.1 In the course of her employment, Employee may process personal data of Company's clients, employees, and other individuals. Employee shall process such personal data only as necessary to perform her duties, in accordance with Company's data protection policies and applicable law, including the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act and, where applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act with respect to Company's California-based clients.

11.2 Employee shall promptly report to Company's Data Protection Officer any actual or suspected data breach of which she becomes aware.

---

## 12. TERMINATION

12.1 **Termination by Company Without Cause**. Company may terminate Employee's employment at any time without Cause upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice, or payment in lieu of notice.

12.2 **Termination by Employee**. Employee may resign at any time upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Company.

12.3 **Termination for Cause**. Company may terminate Employee's employment immediately for "Cause," meaning: (a) Employee's material breach of this Agreement that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days after written notice; (b) Employee's conviction of a felony or crime involving moral turpitude; (c) Employee's gross negligence or willful misconduct in the performance of her duties; or (d) Employee's material violation of Company's Code of Conduct.

12.4 **Resignation for Good Reason**. Employee may resign for "Good Reason" if, without her consent: (a) her Base Salary is reduced by more than ten percent (10%); (b) her position or duties are materially diminished; or (c) Company requires relocation of her principal place of work by more than fifty (50) miles, provided Employee gives Company written notice within sixty (60) days of the event and Company fails to cure within thirty (30) days thereafter.

12.5 **Effect of Termination**. Upon termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall be entitled to payment of accrued and unpaid Base Salary, accrued but unused PTO, and reimbursement of outstanding business expenses, in each case through the date of termination.

---

## 13. SEVERANCE

13.1 If Company terminates Employee's employment without Cause, or Employee resigns for Good Reason, Company shall pay Employee, subject to her execution and non-revocation of a general release of claims, severance equal to six (6) months of her then-current Base Salary, payable in a lump sum within sixty (60) days of termination, together with continuation of health benefits under COBRA for such six (6) month period at Company's expense.

---

## 14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

14.1 **Indemnification of Employee**. Company shall indemnify Employee to the fullest extent permitted by Company's bylaws and applicable Delaware and Illinois law with respect to actions taken by Employee within the scope of her employment and in good faith on behalf of Company.

14.2 **Limitation**. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to limit Employee's statutory rights under applicable wage and hour, anti-discrimination, or labor law.

---

## 15. FORCE MAJEURE

15.1 Neither Party shall be deemed in breach of this Agreement to the extent performance is prevented by a Force Majeure Event, including natural disaster, pandemic, governmental order, or other circumstances beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party, provided that Company's obligation to pay accrued Base Salary shall not be excused except as required by applicable law.

---

## 16. GOVERNING LAW

16.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

## 17. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

17.1 Except for claims for injunctive relief under Section 10, any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including claims relating to termination of employment, shall be resolved through binding **arbitration** administered by JAMS under its Employment Arbitration Rules, before a single arbitrator in Chicago, Illinois.

17.2 Company shall bear all arbitration filing and administrative fees in excess of those Employee would incur if the dispute were filed in court.

17.3 The Parties agree that any arbitration under this Section shall be conducted on an individual basis and not as part of a class, collective, or representative action, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

17.4 Notwithstanding the foregoing, Company may seek **injunctive relief** in the state or federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois, to enforce Sections 8, 9, and 10 of this Agreement.
