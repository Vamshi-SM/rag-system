SCOPE OF AUDIT
This audit evaluated Orion Financial's Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) and AML
compliance program, including customer due diligence (CDD) procedures,
suspicious activity report (SAR) filing practices, and transaction
monitoring systems, in connection with Orion Financial's expanded
investment relationship with Horizon Capital (Contract Number CN-70218,
referenced in shareholder_meeting_minutes.txt).

METHODOLOGY
Bennett & Wolfe Compliance Advisors reviewed a sample of 1,240 transactions
exceeding $10,000, conducted 22 employee interviews, and tested the
effectiveness of Orion Financial's automated transaction monitoring
system, "SentryWatch v4.2."

COMPLIANCE FINDINGS
1. SAR Filing Timeliness: 94% of SARs were filed within the required
   30-day window under 31 U.S.C. § 5318(g); 6% (14 of 233 filings)
   exceeded the deadline by an average of 4.2 days, constituting a
   moderate compliance gap.
2. Customer Due Diligence: Enhanced due diligence (EDD) files for 8 of
   45 sampled high-risk customers were incomplete, missing beneficial
   ownership verification documentation required under 31 C.F.R.
   § 1010.230.
3. Transaction Monitoring: SentryWatch v4.2 flagged 3,412 alerts during
   the audit period; disposition review found appropriate escalation in
   97% of cases, with 3% requiring supplemental documentation.
4. Wire Transfer Screening: OFAC sanctions screening was found to be
   functioning correctly across all sampled wire transactions; no
   sanctioned-party matches identified.
5. Training Records: 12% of relevant employees had not completed the
   annual AML training refresher by the required December 31, 2025
   deadline.
