BACKGROUND
Vertex Manufacturing's Riverbend Production Plant is subject to a National
Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permit (Permit No.
IN0043218) issued under the Clean Water Act. Following a routine IDEM
inspection on January 28, 2026, the facility received a Notice of
Violation (NOV No. IDEM-NOV-2026-0091) citing elevated levels of total
suspended solids (TSS) in wastewater discharge exceeding permitted limits
on four occasions between October 2025 and January 2026.

COMPLIANCE FINDINGS
1. TSS levels recorded at 58 mg/L, 62 mg/L, 71 mg/L, and 66 mg/L against
   a permitted daily maximum of 45 mg/L (Outfall 002).
2. Root cause investigation by Cornerstone Environmental Group identified
   a malfunctioning sediment filtration unit installed in 2019 as the
   primary contributor.
3. No exceedances identified at Outfall 001 or Outfall 003.
4. Facility's Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) Plan
   was last updated in 2022 and is due for its five-year review by
   September 2027; no deficiencies noted in current SPCC audit.
5. Air emissions permit (Title V Permit No. TV-IN-88452) remains in good
   standing; no exceedances reported for calendar year 2025.

RELEVANT STATUTES AND LEGAL REFERENCES
- Clean Water Act, 33 U.S.C. § 1251 et seq.
- 40 C.F.R. Part 122 (NPDES Permit Program)
- Indiana Code Title 13 (Environmental Statutes)
- Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA), 42 U.S.C. § 6901 et seq.

INVESTIGATION NOTES
- Marissa Blake's report (Ref. CEG-2026-0341, dated February 20, 2026)
  recommends immediate replacement of the sediment filtration unit at an
  estimated cost of $185,000, with installation completion targeted for
  May 15, 2026.
- Robert Hayes confirmed interim measures, including increased manual
  sediment removal and reduced production throughput on the affected
  line, were implemented February 5, 2026, resulting in TSS levels
  returning to compliance (38 mg/L recorded February 28, 2026).
- IDEM has indicated it may pursue a civil penalty in the range of
  $15,000 - $40,000 per violation day under Indiana Code § 13-30-4,
  though settlement discussions are ongoing.
- This is Vertex Manufacturing's second NOV in three years; the prior
  NOV (2023) related to unpermitted stormwater discharge and resulted in
  a $22,000 settlement.
