INCIDENT SUMMARY
On March 18, 2026, at approximately 2:14 AM PT, NovaTech Systems' security
operations center detected unauthorized access to a cloud storage bucket
containing source code repositories associated with Project Falconstrike,
the autonomous navigation initiative at the center of the pending IP
dispute with Quantum Robotics (Matter Number MAT-2026-0417). The breach
was initially identified by an automated anomaly-detection alert flagging
unusual data egress of approximately 340 GB.

TIMELINE OF EVENTS
- March 18, 2026, 2:14 AM PT: Anomalous access detected by SIEM system.
- March 18, 2026, 2:47 AM PT: Internal Security Team (lead: Omar Farid,
  CISO) isolates affected storage bucket.
- March 18, 2026, 6:00 AM PT: Incident escalated to Legal (James Kowalski
  notified via emergency contact protocol).
- March 19, 2026: Forensic investigation firm Castellan Forensics engaged
  (Engagement Ref. CSF-88301).
- March 22, 2026: Preliminary forensic findings indicate intrusion vector
  was a compromised third-party contractor credential (contractor: Nexbridge
  IT Services, credential belonging to former contractor ID CNT-4471).
- March 25, 2026: Determination made that personal data of 12,400
  individuals (employee records, not customer data) may have been exposed.
- March 28, 2026: Notification obligations triggered under California
  Civil Code § 1798.82 and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

AFFECTED SYSTEMS AND DATA
- Source code repository: Project Falconstrike (navigation algorithms)
- HR database subset: employee names, employee IDs, partial SSNs
- No customer financial data was affected per current forensic findings.

REGULATORY NOTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
- California Attorney General notification required if over 500 CA
  residents affected (threshold met - notification filed April 1, 2026).
- California Civil Code § 1798.82 requires notice "in the most expedient
  time possible and without unreasonable delay."
- Notification to affected employees mailed March 31, 2026.
- Federal Trade Commission informal inquiry received April 5, 2026,
  Reference No. FTC-INQ-2026-6642.

INVESTIGATION NOTES
- Castellan Forensics report (Ref. CSF-88301-R1, dated March 30, 2026)
  concludes the contractor credential was obtained via a phishing email
  sent to a Nexbridge IT Services employee on March 10, 2026.
- No evidence to date that Quantum Robotics or any competitor received
  the exfiltrated source code; however, this remains under active review
  given the pending IP litigation.
- Internal counsel flagged potential overlap between this incident and
  the ongoing NovaTech v. Quantum Robotics litigation - specifically
  whether the breach could complicate NovaTech's trade secret claims.
- IT Director Omar Farid confirmed multi-factor authentication was not
  enforced on the Nexbridge contractor account at the time of the breach,
  a gap identified in a prior security audit dated September 2025.
