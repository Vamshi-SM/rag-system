NATURE OF DISPUTE
NovaTech Systems alleges that Quantum Robotics, Inc. willfully infringed
U.S. Patent No. 11,982,347 ("Autonomous Navigation Correction System"),
which underlies core technology developed as part of Project Falconstrike,
a joint robotics initiative between NovaTech Systems and Falcon Aerospace.
The patent was granted on March 4, 2025, and covers adaptive path-correction
algorithms used in autonomous ground vehicles.

BACKGROUND
1. NovaTech Systems filed the original patent application (Serial No.
   17/554,209) on September 18, 2023.
2. Quantum Robotics released its "PathSense Pro" navigation module on
   October 2, 2025, allegedly incorporating substantially similar
   correction algorithms.
3. Internal engineering review (memo dated November 14, 2025) concluded
   there is a 78% functional overlap between the two systems.
4. Cease-and-desist letter sent to Quantum Robotics on December 3, 2025;
   no response received within the 30-day window.
