II. CORPORATE STRUCTURE FINDINGS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BluePeak Software, Inc. is a Delaware corporation incorporated on
March 4, 2014. The company has two wholly owned subsidiaries:
- BluePeak Software Canada Ltd. (Ontario)
- BluePeak Analytics UK Limited (England and Wales)

Capitalization: BluePeak Software has issued 8,400,000 shares of common
stock and 2,100,000 shares of Series B Preferred Stock, held primarily by
venture investors Cascade Ventures and Redline Capital Partners. A

transaction structure.

Finding DD-001 (Risk Rating: LOW): Corporate records are well-organized
and consistent; no gaps identified in board minutes or stock issuance
records for the period reviewed (2014-2024).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
III. MATERIAL CONTRACTS REVIEW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Finding DD-002 (Risk Rating: MODERATE): BluePeak Software's Master
Services Agreement with its largest customer, GreenStone Logistics
Corp. (Contract No. BP-GS-2021-0056, representing approximately 11% of
annual recurring revenue), contains a change-of-control termination
right in Section 12.3. GreenStone Logistics may terminate the agreement
upon 60 days' notice following a change of control of BluePeak Software.
Recommend requesting a consent or waiver from GreenStone Logistics prior
to closing.

[Internal Comment - Marcus Chen, 2024-08-02]: Note the coincidental
overlap - GreenStone Logistics is also the defendant in our unrelated
litigation matter for NovaTech Systems (Case ID LC-2045, Matter Number
APX-2024-0113). This is purely coincidental and does not appear to create
a conflict, but flagging for partner awareness given the client
relationship complexity.

Finding DD-003 (Risk Rating: LOW): Cloud hosting agreement with Orion
Financial's data infrastructure affiliate is on standard commercial
terms; no unusual liability caps or indemnification gaps identified.

Finding DD-004 (Risk Rating: MODERATE): Office lease for BluePeak
Software's Austin, TX headquarters (780 Cirrus Way - Suite 400) contains
an assignment clause requiring landlord consent for any change of
control involving more than 50% of outstanding equity. Landlord contact
identified: Meridian Property Group, Attn: Legal Department.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IV. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY FINDINGS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Finding DD-005 (Risk Rating: LOW): BluePeak Software holds three issued
U.S. patents (Patent Nos. 10,234,551; 10,887,220; and 11,092,447) related
to predictive logistics routing algorithms, and 14 registered trademarks.
No pending infringement claims identified in USPTO TSDR or PACER searches
conducted August 12, 2024.

Finding DD-006 (Risk Rating: MODERATE): Open-source software audit
identified two components (a logging library and a data serialization
library) licensed under copyleft licenses (GPL v3) that were not properly
segregated from proprietary code per BluePeak's own internal open-source
policy. Recommend remediation prior to closing or a specific
indemnification provision in the acquisition agreement addressing this
risk.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
V. LITIGATION AND REGULATORY HISTORY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Finding DD-007 (Risk Rating: LOW): No pending or threatened litigation
involving BluePeak Software identified other than a resolved 2022 wage
and hour class action (settled for $340,000, fully paid, no ongoing
obligations).

Finding DD-008 (Risk Rating: MODERATE): BluePeak Software received an
informal inquiry letter from the Texas Attorney General's office dated
May 14, 2024, requesting information about data retention practices for
former customer accounts. BluePeak Software's General Counsel, Priya
Anand, represented that the inquiry is preliminary and no formal
investigation has been opened. Recommend obtaining a written
representation and specific indemnification provision covering this
matter in the definitive agreement.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VI. EMPLOYMENT AND BENEFITS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Finding DD-009 (Risk Rating: LOW): Standard employment agreements and
benefit plans reviewed; no material unfunded pension liabilities
identified. 340 employees as of August 1, 2024, with 12 employees subject
to retention agreements that will require renegotiation or assumption at
closing.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VII. DATA PRIVACY AND CYBERSECURITY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Finding DD-010 (Risk Rating: MODERATE): BluePeak Software maintains
SOC 2 Type II certification (most recent audit period ending March 31,
2024) but has not yet completed ISO 27001 certification. BluePeak
Software also serves as a subprocessor for Redwood Healthcare's
telehealth platform (Project Meridian); GDPR-related data processing
addendum was reviewed and found generally consistent with Article 28
requirements, though it lacks specific Standard Contractual Clauses for
transfers to the Canadian subsidiary. See Legal Research Notes (Matter
Number APX-2024-0188) for related GDPR analysis prepared for Redwood
Healthcare.

Finding DD-011 (Risk Rating: HIGH): A security incident in November 2023
resulted in unauthorized access to a non-production test database
containing anonymized customer records. BluePeak Software notified
affected customers per its incident response policy; no regulatory
penalties were assessed. However, remediation documentation is
incomplete, and recommend obtaining a full incident response report and
specific representations regarding data security prior to closing.
