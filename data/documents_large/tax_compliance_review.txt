BACKGROUND
Summit Energy Group entered into a joint venture agreement with Meridian
Energy on June 15, 2025, to develop a renewable natural gas facility in
Beaumont, Texas (Contract Number CN-77410, "Beaumont RNG Project"). This
review evaluates the tax treatment of the joint venture structure and
Summit Energy Group's broader federal and state tax compliance posture
for tax year 2025.

SCOPE OF REVIEW
1. Federal income tax return preparation and filing accuracy (Form 1120).
2. Section 45Z clean fuel production tax credit eligibility analysis for
   the Beaumont RNG Project.
3. Texas franchise tax compliance.
4. Transfer pricing documentation for intercompany transactions with
   Summit Energy Group's Canadian subsidiary, Summit Energy Canada ULC.

FINDINGS
1. Federal Return: Form 1120 for tax year 2025 was filed timely on
   March 15, 2026, reporting total taxable income of $128,400,000 and
   federal tax liability of $26,964,000 (21% statutory rate).
2. Clean Fuel Production Credit: The Beaumont RNG Project qualifies for
   the Section 45Z credit under the Inflation Reduction Act; estimated
   credit value for 2025 is $4,200,000, contingent on final EPA
   lifecycle emissions certification (pending as of report date).
3. Texas Franchise Tax: Compliance confirmed; no deficiencies identified.
4. Transfer Pricing: Fenwick Tax Advisory Group identified a documentation
   gap in the intercompany services agreement between Summit Energy Group
   and Summit Energy Canada ULC; contemporaneous transfer pricing study
   was not completed for the 2025 tax year as required under 26 U.S.C.
   § 482 and corresponding Treasury Regulations.
