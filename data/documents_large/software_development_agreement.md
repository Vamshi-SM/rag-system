If the breach is not reasonably capable of cure within the Cure Period, the breaching Party shall not be in default if it commences cure within the Cure Period and diligently pursues such cure to completion within an additional thirty (30) days.

15.4 **Termination for Insolvency**. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy protection, or has a receiver appointed over a substantial part of its assets.

15.5 **Effect of Termination**. Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement: (a) Developer shall deliver to Client all Deliverables and work product completed or in progress as of the termination date, subject to payment of amounts due therefor; (b) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information upon request; and (c) Sections 6, 7.4, 9, 12, 13, 16, 17, 19, and 20 shall survive termination.
