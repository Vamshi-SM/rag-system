NATURE OF DISPUTE
Claimant Rachel Simmons alleges wrongful termination, retaliation, and
gender-based pay discrimination in violation of the California Fair
Employment and Housing Act (FEHA) and the federal Equal Pay Act, 29 U.S.C.
§ 206(d). Simmons was terminated on January 9, 2026, approximately six
weeks after filing an internal complaint regarding compensation disparity
on the Project Ironclad compliance overhaul team.

BACKGROUND
1. Simmons joined BluePeak Software on May 3, 2021, as a Senior Product
   Manager assigned to Project Ironclad.
2. On November 20, 2025, Simmons submitted an internal complaint to HR
   (Ref. HR-COMP-2025-0977) alleging pay disparity relative to a male
   colleague in an equivalent role.
3. Linda Park initiated an internal review; findings (dated December 18,
   2025) identified a $14,200 annual salary gap not fully explained by
   performance ratings or tenure.
4. Simmons was terminated on January 9, 2026, cited for "restructuring of
   the Project Ironclad team."
5. Two other employees on the same team, including Employee ID EMP-30291
   (name withheld per settlement), were retained.
