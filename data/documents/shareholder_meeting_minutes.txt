SHAREHOLDERS REPRESENTED
Approximately 87.4% of outstanding shares were represented in person or
by proxy, satisfying the quorum requirement under Horizon Capital's
Amended and Restated Bylaws, Article III, Section 4.

CALL TO ORDER
The meeting was called to order at 10:04 AM CT by Chairman Walter
Higgins, who confirmed the presence of a quorum as certified by
Corporate Secretary Nathaniel Cross.

ITEM 1: APPROVAL OF PRIOR MINUTES
The minutes of the March 20, 2025 Annual Meeting were approved unanimously
without amendment.

ITEM 2: FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE REPORT
CFO Jennifer Adams presented the fiscal year 2025 financial results.
Total revenue for FY2025 was reported at $412,600,000, representing a
9.2% increase over FY2024. Net income was reported at $58,300,000.
Adams noted that Horizon Capital's strategic investment position in
Orion Financial, established through the Q3 2025 transaction (Contract
Number CN-70218), contributed approximately $6,100,000 in unrealized
gains during the reporting period.

ITEM 3: ELECTION OF DIRECTORS
Shareholders voted to re-elect the following directors for a one-year
term: Walter Higgins, Jennifer Adams, Dr. Monique Fairweather, Theodore
Baptiste, and Grace Liu. Voting results:
- Walter Higgins: 94.1% in favor
- Jennifer Adams: 96.3% in favor
- Dr. Monique Fairweather: 91.8% in favor
- Theodore Baptiste: 89.2% in favor
- Grace Liu: 95.0% in favor

ITEM 4: RATIFICATION OF INDEPENDENT AUDITOR
Shareholders ratified the appointment of Caldwell & Marsh LLP as
independent auditor for fiscal year 2026, with 97.6% in favor.

ITEM 5: EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION (SAY-ON-PAY)
The advisory vote on executive compensation passed with 82.4% approval.
Several proxy advisory firms had flagged concerns regarding CEO Walter
Higgins' 2025 equity award; the Compensation Committee, chaired by
Theodore Baptiste, committed to reviewing the equity grant structure
ahead of the 2027 proxy statement.

ITEM 6: PROPOSED MERGER DISCUSSION - INFORMATIONAL UPDATE
Sarah Whitfield provided a legal update on Horizon Capital's preliminary
discussions regarding a potential minority investment in Alpha
Infrastructure. No formal transaction has been announced; discussions
remain in early due diligence phase under a mutual non-disclosure
agreement executed February 1, 2026 (NDA Reference NDA-HC-2026-0044).
Shareholders were reminded that any material transaction would be
subject to a separate special meeting and applicable disclosure
requirements under Securities Exchange Act Rule 13e-3, if applicable.

ITEM 7: SHAREHOLDER PROPOSAL - CLIMATE RISK DISCLOSURE
A shareholder proposal requesting enhanced climate risk disclosure in
Horizon Capital's annual report was put to a vote. The proposal received
41.2% support, falling short of majority approval. The Board noted it
will continue to evaluate voluntary climate disclosure enhancements.

LEGAL COUNSEL REMARKS
Sarah Whitfield reminded the Board of ongoing compliance obligations
under the Sarbanes-Oxley Act, 15 U.S.C. § 7201 et seq., and confirmed
that Horizon Capital's Q1 2026 internal controls certification remains
on schedule for filing by May 10, 2026. Whitfield also noted that
Horizon Capital's outstanding advisory relationship with Sterling
Consulting (Contract Number CN-48802) for enterprise risk management
services was renewed through December 2027.

ACTION ITEMS

   8-K) within four business days per Item 5.07 requirements.
2. Compensation Committee to report back on equity grant structure
   review by Q1 2027 Annual Meeting.
3. Legal team to continue monitoring Alpha Infrastructure due diligence

4. Nathaniel Cross to circulate final approved minutes to all directors

ADJOURNMENT
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 12:17 PM CT
upon motion by Theodore Baptiste, seconded by Grace Liu, and approved
unanimously.
