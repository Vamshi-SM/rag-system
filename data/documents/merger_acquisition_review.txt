II. DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT KEY TERMS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stock Purchase Agreement dated August 14, 2024 between NovaTech Systems,
Inc. and the selling stockholders of BluePeak Software, Inc., including
Cascade Ventures and Redline Capital Partners as majority Series B
Preferred stockholders.

Key Provisions:
- Section 2.1: Purchase price mechanics and working capital true-up
  (90-day post-closing adjustment period)
- Section 4.6: Representations regarding intellectual property, including
  specific disclosure schedule item addressing the open-source licensing
  issue identified in Due Diligence Finding DD-006
- Section 6.2: Closing conditions, including receipt of GreenStone
  Logistics consent under Contract No. BP-GS-2021-0056 and landlord
  consent from Meridian Property Group
- Section 8.3: Indemnification provisions, capped at the escrow amount
  except for fraud, willful misconduct, and breaches of fundamental
  representations (capped at full purchase price)
- Section 9.1: Non-compete and non-solicitation covenants for key
  BluePeak Software executives, including Robert Nakamura (CEO) and
  Priya Anand (General Counsel), for a period of 3 years post-closing

III. CLOSING CONDITIONS STATUS TRACKER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Condition 1: GreenStone Logistics consent to change of control under
Contract No. BP-GS-2021-0056
Status: RESOLVED - Consent obtained September 30, 2024, following
negotiation of revised pricing terms favorable to BluePeak Software.

Condition 2: Landlord consent from Meridian Property Group for Austin,
TX headquarters lease assignment
Status: RESOLVED - Consent obtained September 18, 2024.

Condition 3: Repayment or assumption of existing BluePeak Software term
loan (Contract No. OF-TL-2020-0091, outstanding balance $8,000,000) with
Orion Financial
Status: RESOLVED - Loan will be repaid at closing from transaction
proceeds; payoff letter received October 1, 2024.

Condition 4: HSR Act antitrust waiting period expiration
Status: RESOLVED - Early termination granted September 5, 2024 (waiting
period would otherwise have expired October 4, 2024).

Condition 5: Resolution or indemnification of Texas Attorney General
inquiry (Due Diligence Finding DD-008)
Status: RESOLVED VIA INDEMNIFICATION - Specific indemnification provision
added to Section 8.4 of the Stock Purchase Agreement; Texas Attorney
General's office confirmed on September 12, 2024 that its inquiry has
been closed without further action.

Condition 6: Security incident remediation documentation (Due Diligence
Finding DD-011)
Status: RESOLVED - BluePeak Software's Chief Information Security
Officer, Tanya Brooks, delivered a complete remediation report on
September 27, 2024, confirming all identified vulnerabilities were
patched and a third-party penetration test (conducted by Sterling
Consulting) confirmed no residual exposure.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
V. VALUATION SUMMARY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Valuation performed by Sterling Consulting, Financial Advisory Division,
using a combination of discounted cash flow analysis and comparable
transaction multiples (comparable transactions in the supply chain
analytics software sector averaged 6.2x trailing twelve-month revenue).

BluePeak Software Fiscal Year 2023 Revenue: $34,200,000
BluePeak Software Fiscal Year 2023 EBITDA: $6,850,000
Implied Multiple at $128,000,000 Enterprise Value: 3.7x revenue, 18.7x
EBITDA

Sterling Consulting's fairness opinion, delivered to the NovaTech Systems
Board on October 1, 2024, concluded that the purchase price is fair to
NovaTech Systems from a financial point of view.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open-source remediation (Due Diligence Finding DD-006) to be completed
   within 90 days post-closing under supervision of NovaTech Systems'
   engineering leadership, with legal check-ins at 30 and 60 days.
